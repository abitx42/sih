Terms of Use

Truth Lens · Forensic Darkroom · Read carefully before proceeding

1. Authorised Use Only

Truth Lens is designed for law enforcement, digital forensic professionals, journalists, and authorised researchers. You may only analyse evidence you are legally authorised to process. Unauthorised use is strictly prohibited and may be reported to relevant authorities.

2. Privacy & Data Storage

Files you upload are processed locally on-device. No image data is sent to external cloud services during analysis. Your email is used only for authentication and is never sold, shared, or used for marketing. We do not send spam — ever.

3. AI Model Training Consent

To improve detection accuracy over time, anonymised forensic signals (model confidence scores, heuristic outputs — never raw images) derived from submissions may be used for model improvement. Raw files are never used for training without explicit opt-in. No PII is retained for training purposes. You can withdraw consent any time in Account Settings.

4. Forensic Disclaimer

Results from Truth Lens are statistical indicators and investigator aids — not legal proof. All findings must be reviewed by a qualified human forensic examiner before use in legal, judicial, or official proceedings. The platform makes no warranty of detection accuracy.

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation

In cases involving credible threats to public safety or active criminal investigations, Truth Lens may cooperate with lawful requests from authorities in accordance with the IT Act 2000/2008 and applicable international law. Users will be notified wherever legally permitted.

6. Prohibited Content

You must not submit CSAM, content violating the IT Act, or material you lack lawful authority to process. Violations result in immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement.

7. Limitation of Liability

Truth Lens is provided on a best-effort basis. The development team is not liable for decisions made based on automated forensic outputs. Human expert review is always required for consequential decisions.
